Noita eye messages

The nine eye messages are unsolved. These pages collect what can be proved about the cipher, which keys have been searched, and what the repeats say about the text. Each result names the assumption it needs. The reading of the glyphs and the transcription are under Basics & data.

Zero doubles: 1 in 20, not 1 in 230,000

No symbol is followed by itself in 1,018 adjacent pairs. If the pairs were independent draws from 83 symbols, that would happen once in 230,000 tries. They are not independent: with a fixed key, a given pair of letters either always makes a double or never does. In 400 random class-shuffle keys applied to English text, 4.8% produced no doubles. The condition and the experiment

A trillion seeded keys, no hit

The eyes first shipped between the builds of 15 July and 15 October 2020. Every second of that window was tried as a clock seed for MSVC, glibc and LuaJIT generators, across thousands of key layouts each. The best key decrypts 44 of 918 cells; a true key decrypts all of them. What was searched and what was not

The text looks like records, not prose

E1, W1 and E2 each contain one phrase twice, 28, 30 and 35 cells apart, after a shared opening and a short stretch that differs. Verse laid out in reading order gave several messages that open alike in at most 12% of trials; a record written out three times with different numbers did in all of them. What is not known

The key, the alphabet, the language, and whether the text has spaces. The class-shuffle model reproduces the shared openings, the isomorphs and the way messages agree again after differing, and no key for it has been found. A wrong guess of the text can pass every quick check: 3 of 120 did in one test. A solution is a key that re-encrypts the text to all nine messages.
