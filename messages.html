Basics & data

The reading of the glyphs, the transcription, and the shared openings. The Noita wiki has the locations and screenshots.

Reading the eyes

The messages are walls of eye glyphs in the first parallel worlds to the east and the west: five in the east, E1 to E5, and four in the west, W1 to W4. The numbering is the community's. These pages list them in the order E1, W1, E2, W2, E3, W3, E4, W4, E5, which is the order the game's executable stores them in. Each eye looks centre, up, right, down or left, read as the digits 0 to 4. The eyes sit in rows of up to 39, and each pair of rows interlocks into triangles of three eyes, alternately pointing up and down. The first cells of E1:

Hover over a triangle to number its eyes in reading order. Under each triangle are its three digits in that order and, below them, the symbol: 2, 0, 0 gives 2 × 25 + 0 × 5 + 0 = 50. A triangle pointing up is read as a three-digit base-5 number: top left, top right, then the eye below. A triangle pointing down is the same shape turned upside down, and its reading order turns with it: bottom right, bottom left, then the eye above. The eye directions themselves are not turned. The 1,036 decoded symbols use exactly 0 to 82, though three base-5 digits could encode 0 to 124.

A cell is one triangle, and so one symbol. Cells are numbered from 0 in each message, along the first pair of rows and then the next pair. "E1 30–48" means cells 30 to 48 of E1.

With the up triangles fixed, there are 24 ways to read a down triangle: six orders of its three eyes, with the eye directions kept or swapped in pairs one of three ways. Only this reading gives all of: values filling 0–82 with no gaps, no symbol next to itself, and no conflict in twelve isomorph pairs (stretches that share a pattern of repeats). The decoder rebuilds all nine messages from the eye rows.

Lymm's wiki covers the reading and the alternatives that were tried.

The transcription

The nine messages hold 99, 103, 118, 102, 137, 124, 119, 120 and 114 symbols. Hovering over a symbol highlights its other occurrences in the message. Symbols run from 0 to 82, twenty to a line, with the cell number of the first symbol at the left. eyes.json contains these numbers and the raw eye directions. The openings sort the messages into three groups, which the other pages refer to: E1, W1 and E2; W2, E3 and W3; E4, W4 and E5. The first and last groups share long openings. The middle group shares only cells 1–5 and is held together by an isomorph that starts at W2 18, E3 24 and W3 23.

Cell 0 and the counts used on these pages

Cell 0 holds 50, 80, 36, 76, 63, 34, 27, 77 and 33, in the order E1, W1, E2, W2, E3, W3, E4, W4, E5. Read as three eye digits, the middle digit of each message is the first digit of the previous message minus one, in 7 of the 8 steps (found by Dr Cats, on the Noita Discord). It is the best of 81 relations of this kind, and in shuffled orders the best of the 81 reaches 7 of 8 only 1.4% of the time. So cell 0 most likely numbers the messages, and the rest of these pages leave it out of the cipher. All nine messages agree at cells 1 and 2 whatever cell 0 holds.
