Basics & data

Nine walls of eye glyphs, still unsolved. The Noita wiki has their locations and screenshots. This page collects the reading convention and reference data; the front page covers the research results.

Reading the eyes

The messages are walls of eye glyphs in the first parallel worlds to the east and the west: five in the east, E1 to E5, and four in the west, W1 to W4. The numbering is the community's. Each eye looks centre, up, right, down or left, read as the digits 0 to 4. The eyes sit in rows of up to 39, and each pair of rows interlocks into triangles of three eyes, alternately pointing up and down. The first cells of E1:

A triangle pointing up is read as a three-digit base-5 number: top left, top right, then the eye below. A triangle pointing down is the same shape turned upside down, and its reading order turns with it: bottom right, bottom left, then the eye above. The eye directions themselves are not turned. The 1,036 decoded symbols use exactly 0 to 82, though three base-5 digits could encode 0 to 124. Cell numbers on this site count from 0.

An orientation audit kept the up triangles fixed and tested six digit orders × four direction mappings for the down triangles. Only the conventional reading retained all three tested features: the contiguous 0–82 range, no adjacent doubles, and consistency of 12 selected isomorph pairs. This compares those 24 readings, not every possible way to group eyes. The decoder checks the adopted reading against all nine transcriptions.

The grouping and deck-cipher hypotheses are developed in Lymm's wiki. An eye-reading convention is not itself a decryption; the class-shuffle model begins with the resulting 83 symbols.

Cell 0 holds 50, 80, 36, 76, 63, 34, 27, 77 and 33, in the order E1, W1, E2, W2, E3, W3, E4, W4, E5. Read as three eye digits, the middle digit of each message is the first digit of the previous message minus one, in 7 of the 8 steps. In the ordering test, 1.4% of shuffled orders had some relation among the 81 tested digit/offset rules reach 7 of 8. Dr Cats reported the chain on the Noita Discord. It suggests ordering metadata, but does not establish a unique order or prove that cell 0 is unencrypted. The model and body statistics here exclude cell 0; the transcription includes it.
